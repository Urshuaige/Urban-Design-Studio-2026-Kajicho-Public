# Kajicho studio timeline

Theme B studies how to renew the built fabric of Kajicho 1 & 2, within the wider Kanda–Nihonbashi area, while reducing emissions, adapting to heat and flooding, and improving the public realm.

[Studio exhibition](https://kajicho-site-atlas.specialgooood.chatgpt.site/studio.html) · [Site Atlas](https://kajicho-site-atlas.specialgooood.chatgpt.site/) · [Source map and GIS package](../04_Data/Ready/QGIS_Current_2026-11-04/README.md)

| Date | Milestone | What came out of it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 Oct 2026 | Common foundation | A first QGIS base, a shared study boundary and a data register. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Studio kick-off | Theme B set out to build an area management model for decarbonization and climate resilience. |
| 25 Oct 2026 | Kajicho fieldwork | The team walked the study area and spoke with stakeholders about local streets, businesses and buildings. [Field visit](https://kajicho-site-atlas.specialgooood.chatgpt.site/field-visit.html) |
| 29 Oct–4 Nov 2026 | Shared maps and analysis | The team combined land use, buildings and streets in QGIS. The wider GIS delivery retains complete building footprints across the boundary. |
| 4 Nov 2026 | Georgia Tech review | The team presented its site findings and early design ideas. |
| 13–14 Nov 2026 | Building-age update | The primary age layer was refreshed. It records 221 supplied years out of 277 buildings. [Building age](https://kajicho-site-atlas.specialgooood.chatgpt.site/building-age.html) |
| 18 Nov 2026 | Interim presentation | Six studies contributed to *Carrying Kajicho Forward*, a working vision for the next design phase. [Browse the results](https://kajicho-site-atlas.specialgooood.chatgpt.site/studio.html#studies) |
| 16 Dec 2026 | Final presentation (scheduled) | Refine the design and implementation approach using interim feedback, then assemble the final presentation and booklet. |
