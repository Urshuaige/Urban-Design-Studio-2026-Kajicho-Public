# Typography and map-colour sources

Chiyoda's public-signage manual uses dark grey as a main colour and Edo purple as an accent. The portfolio combines paper white, dark text and purple chapter lines. Map categories retain their MLIT legend meanings.

## Local references

[Chiyoda public-signage manual, chapter 3](https://www.city.chiyoda.lg.jp/documents/4204/designmanual-3.pdf#page=5), printed pp.32–33: dark grey C0 M2 Y0 K68 / PANTONE Cool Gray 11C; Edo purple C78 M100 Y0 K33 / PANTONE 269C. Website `#59457F` is a studio digital approximation.

Printed p.36 lists Hiragino Kaku Gothic and Frutiger as basic sign typefaces, allowing Mincho or brush lettering for distinctive cultural-property signs. Retro serif titles and readable body, table and formula fonts are the studio's composition choice.

## Provenance

Colours were checked against official webpages and PDFs; relevant pages were rendered for internal review. Published maps/legends are independently drawn and cite data and guidance. The Chiyoda crest is not part of the design. Retrieval and organisation used AI assistance; screen HEX values, layout and font combinations are studio decisions.
