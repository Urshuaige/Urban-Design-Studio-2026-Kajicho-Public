Full rows and location/matching provenance: `Traffic_Source_Matching.json`, `data/Traffic_Sections.geojson`, and `data/Traffic_Baseline.gpkg`. Exact counter coordinates were not invented. Colored roads represent matched portions of the official survey sections. Chuo/Showa are clipped at the existing Chiyoda ward boundary and Yasukuni junctions; Yasukuni betweenR17/R4 junctions. Main grey road network continues independently of the statistical overlay.

Road widths and functional classes were not used as flow estimates. Internal roads remain a pale continuous base with no assigned low-flow category. Per latest user direction, explanation of missing local counts is kept here rather than filling the presentation with warnings.
