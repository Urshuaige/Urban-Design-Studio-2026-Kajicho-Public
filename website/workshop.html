What we discussed

Buildings and phasing

The group discussed putting more floor area where it fits and testing lower buildings in parts of Blocks 4 and 5. Recent or usable buildings could stay in service until a later phase.

Streets and access

The group proposed drawing walking, cycling, planting and drainage together for two priority streets. It discussed possible links toward Shōwa-dōri and the Kanda River.

Open space and roofs

The group discussed ground-level squares and planting, and how roof area, sunlight and building renewal affect solar panels, greenery and rainwater storage.

Land rights and local shops

The discussion covered who can approve redevelopment, who would maintain shared space and where shops could operate during construction or return afterward.

Prepare the next draft

Prepare a shared base map, a sketch for each block option and one comparison sheet.

  1. Set street space

    Mark road widths and draw the uses that share each cross-section.

  2. Reserve open space

    Set aside ground space for squares, paths and planting.

  3. Sketch building options

    Estimate building footprints, storeys and total floor area. Mark the roof uses.

  4. Compare combinations

    Draw a few block combinations on one base map and test them against a shared baseline.
