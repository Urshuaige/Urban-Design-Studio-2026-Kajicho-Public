The studies and dates on this site are from the 18 November 2026 interim presentation by Theme B, Urban Design Studio 2026. Page references follow the presentation file order.

Maps and study figures

  • Development and heritage maps, pages 9–10, are by Team B. Basemap sources are GSI, MLIT PLATEAU and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Project evidence comes from Chiyoda City, developers and Shutoko. GSI · PLATEAU · OpenStreetMap
  • The facility inventory and map are by Jiaxiang Zhao, page 15, using OpenStreetMap and the project inventory of 25 October 2026. Solar potential, renewal and energy sharing are by Asuka Tanzawa, pages 22, 25 and 27.
  • Surface temperature, vegetation and greening priorities are by Regina Aura Delazenitha, pages 30–31, using August 2026 Landsat 8 imagery and OpenStreetMap.   • Street sections and the railway-crossing image analysis are by Shair Muhammad, pages 36–41. Relative brightness reflects daytime image samples. The proposed cycle track is a design option.
  • Station access and street perception maps are by Dingchao Shi, pages 43 and 47–49. Network data use © OpenStreetMap contributors, with boundary work dated 29 October 2026. Google Street View imagery informed the street-level observations. OpenStreetMap attribution
