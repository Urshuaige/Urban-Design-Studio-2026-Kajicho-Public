    07 / AFTER THE VISIT

    Turn encounters into a shared question

    1. Place the evidence: attach a location, time and source to each note.
    2. Compare three things: everyday places, activity networks and comfort needs.
    3. Choose one useful question with the group; use the work plan to decide whether shade or wind calculations would help.

    A photograph can document an arrangement or a story. Geometry and environmental calculations still need scale, dimensions and checked model inputs.
