# Baseline team handoff

Agree roles as a team. Join deliveries through `building_id`, date, source and units.

| Role | First task | Deliverable | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GIS lead | Claim the dated project, check 277 core IDs, maintain CRS and main version | GeoPackage, QGIS project, source register | Member files join and relative paths resolve |
| Planning | Review 26 cross-FAR objects against official zones, district plans and frontage | FAR review, legal-site form, policy-response matrix | Official ward-map link, date and reasoning per object |
| Field | Locate individual trees along seven core inventory routes; survey height, crown, street width, shade devices and materials | Separate tree GeoPackage and photo index | Location, method, date and photo per tree; unmeasured values empty |
| Energy | Receive registered DECC data; select by use, region and year; distinguish electricity and primary energy | Low/mid/high EUI template and unit checks | Traceable originals/conversions; scenarios distinct from bills |
| Climate | Check two summer sample days, inflow/solar boundaries, coordinates and wind origin | Boundary table and mesh/solver notes | Repeatable baseline/option comparisons with numerical and sensitivity checks |
| Design / story | Select specific streets from exposure, tree gaps and use; define measures and metrics | Baseline maps, option-change table, presentation script | Each action linked to a place, policy and measurable outcome |

Complete source checks and one field review before agreeing an action mix. Small pilots can separately examine energy retrofit, street greenery and basement resilience. Record meetings in `07_Team/Meetings`. One editor writes the main GIS file; other members return separate copies. The current stage adds no CFD runs.
